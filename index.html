    Step-by-Step Guidance

    The High Court Probate Process

    How an estate application progresses from initial filing to asset distribution in Trinidad & Tobago.

    1

    Asset Inventory

    Gather death certificate, bank statements, land deeds, vehicle certified copies, and valuation reports.

    2

    Application Drafting

    Attorney drafts sworn Affidavits (Form 1 or Form 5), Probate Filing Forms, and Caveat Search requests.

    3

    High Court E-Filing

    Application is lodged at the Supreme Court Probate Registry. Newspaper & Gazette advertisements are published.

    4

    Grant Issued

    Upon Registrar satisfaction and query clearance, the official legal Grant is signed and sealed by the Court.

    5

    Asset Assent

    Executor/Administrator closes bank accounts, pays debts, and executes Deeds of Assent to transfer property.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Answers to common probate concerns in Trinidad & Tobago.

    Virtual consultations are conducted via Zoom or Google Meet. You can securely send scanned copies of wills, death certificates, and deeds prior to the call. Counsel will review your documents during the meeting, advise on the appropriate High Court application, and map out the required steps without requiring you to travel to an office.
    Under Trinidad & Tobago law, if the value of the deceased’s estate exceeds $4,800.00 TT, an application for a Grant of Probate or Letters of Administration must be prepared and lodged through an Attorney-at-Law admitted to practice in the Supreme Court.
    The timeline depends on High Court Registry backlogs, query resolutions, and document accuracy. Uncontested, straightforward applications generally take several months from e-filing. Ensuring all initial documents (death certificate, inventory values, deeds) are clean on submission reduces query delays significantly.
    Generally, commercial banks in Trinidad & Tobago freeze sole accounts upon notification of death until a certified copy of the Grant of Probate or Letters of Administration is presented. Certain small sums or funeral expense disbursements may be considered under specific bank policies, but full access requires the Court Grant.
    Heirs or beneficiaries abroad can execute necessary consents, renunciations, or Power of Attorney documents before a Notary Public in their place of residence. L. Thomas & Associates handles the remote notarization verification and High Court filings seamlessly via virtual appointments.
